VPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the VPlay app ("VPlay", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. VPlay is a video player that plays the videos stored on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

VPlay needs permission to access the videos on your device so it can find and play them. Your videos are never uploaded to our servers or shared with us. The app also saves a few settings on your device only, such as your favourite videos, sort and view preferences, and the playback position of videos so you can resume where you left off. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or the content of your videos.

3. Advertising

VPlay shows ads using Huawei Ads (HUAWEI Ads Kit). To show and measure ads, the Huawei Ads service may collect and process information such as your device information and advertising identifier. This information is handled by Huawei under its own privacy policy, available at https://consumer.huawei.com/en/legal/privacy-policy/. We do not control how Huawei processes this data.

4. Permissions

The app requests permission to read videos on your device. This is used only to list and play your videos. The app may also use internet access to load ads.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The only third-party service integrated in the app that may process data is Huawei Ads, as described above.

6. Children's privacy

VPlay is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data security

Your favourites and settings are stored locally on your device, and your videos are never sent to us. Please keep your device secure to protect your data.

8. Your choices

You can revoke the video access permission at any time in your device settings, and you can delete all data saved by the app by clearing the app data or uninstalling it. You can also manage ad personalization in your device or Huawei account settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
